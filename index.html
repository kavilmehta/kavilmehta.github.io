01 / About

KM

Dr. Kavil Mehta

Physicist · Taipei, Taiwan

Dr. Kavil Mehta is a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Leung Center for Cosmology and Particle Astrophysics (LeCosPA), National Taiwan University, Taipei, working under the guidance of renowned scientist Prof. Pisin Chen. His research focuses on ultra-intense laser–matter interactions, laser-driven particle acceleration and plasma-based secondary radiation sources.

Before joining LeCosPA, he was a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Atomic, Molecular and Optical Physics Division of the Physical Research Laboratory (PRL), Ahmedabad, under the guidance of Dr. Prashant Kumar, where he developed a Thomson parabola spectrometer to diagnose ions driven by ultrahigh-intensity lasers.

He completed his Ph.D. at Pandit Deendayal Energy University under Dr. Prahlad Kumar Baruah, studying the dynamics of laser ablation in liquid for nanoparticle synthesis.

02 / Research

From a single laser shot to plasma, radiation, particles and new materials.

Laser–plasma

Ultra-intense laser–matter interaction

How intense, short laser pulses ionise solid targets and form dense plasmas that emit high-energy radiation.

Particle diagnostics

Laser-driven particle acceleration

Ions and electrons accelerated by ultrahigh-intensity lasers, and the instruments, such as a Thomson parabola spectrometer, that measure their species and energy.

Spectroscopy

Laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy

Identifying and quantifying elements, including trace elements in liquids, from the light a laser plasma emits.

Imaging

Time-resolved plasma diagnostics

Gated ICCD imaging, Echelle spectroscopy and shadowgraphy that capture a plume's evolution frame by frame.

Nanomaterials

Nanoparticles by laser ablation in liquid

Cavitation-bubble dynamics, confinement, viscosity and external fields, and how they shape the nanoparticles that form.
